# ISEYC institutional identity system

**Working design standard · November 2026**

This standard covers the ISEYC institutional website, Digital Operations Centre, Civic Mandate Nigeria, Civic Brain and Civic Intelligence Observatory.

## Core identity

- Full name: Initiative for Sustainable Evolution for Youth and Community
- Short name: ISEYC
- Tagline: Empowering Youth, Shaping Communities
- Character: calm, evidence-led, non-partisan, accountable, youth-centred and nationally grounded.

## Official mark

Use `/iseyc-seal.svg` as the supplied official seal. Do not redraw, recolour, crop, distort, add effects to, or replace it with generated artwork. Preserve its aspect ratio and clear space.

Use `/iseyc-brand-lockup.svg` for horizontal identity placements. It references the same official seal and pairs it with the full institutional name and tagline. Use the seal-only asset for favicons and compact mobile placements.

## Shared visual language

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Forest | `#103B2E` | Primary institutional identity |
| Deep forest | `#072A20` | Navigation and high-contrast surfaces |
| Warm gold | `#B89A58` | Restrained accent, rules and focus details |
| Paper | `#FAF9F5` | Main light canvas |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and evidence surfaces |
| Ink | `#202923` | Primary text |
| Muted ink | `#626D65` | Secondary labels |

Use a restrained editorial serif for major institutional titles and a legible sans-serif for interface labels, data, forms and body copy. Avoid long all-caps paragraphs.

## Product roles

- **Civic Mandate Nigeria:** citizen-facing civic learning, rights, responsibilities, observations and published demands.
- **Civic Brain:** evidence retrieval, interpretation, institutional routing and human-reviewed briefs.
- **Civic Intelligence Observatory:** national evidence catalogue, source provenance, geography and exploration.
- **Digital Operations Centre:** internal institutional execution, department workspaces, approvals and operational registers.

All departments share the parent identity. Secondary accents may distinguish departments, but must not create competing logos.

## Evidence and interface rules

- Show publisher, reference period, geography, measurement type and limitations beside material statistics.
- Distinguish community reports, administrative records, surveys, estimates, modelled data and analysis.
- Never present an announcement as implementation, a published record as resolution, or a national value as a state/LGA value.
- Never fill missing data with zero or fabricate activity, source freshness, verification or approval.
- Use charts only when data, denominator and period are clear.
- Prefer compact evidence cards, maps, comparisons and concise status labels over explanatory paragraphs.
- Every visible control must work or be clearly marked unavailable.

## Release checklist

- Seal and lockup render correctly on light and dark surfaces.
- Mobile navigation, touch targets and contrast are accessible.
- Product links resolve to the intended product and department.
- Source links, dates and limitations are visible.
- No placeholder metrics, fake records or decorative non-functional controls.
- Preview, runtime integrations, privacy and permission boundaries are verified before production.
