# ISEYC Institutional Brand System

**Status:** Working implementation standard  
**Institution:** Initiative for Sustainable Evolution for Youth and Community  
**Primary tagline:** Empowering Youth, Shaping Communities  
**Civic intelligence line:** Understanding Public Needs. Interpreting Evidence. Strengthening Accountability.

## 1. Identity architecture

ISEYC is the parent institutional identity. The four departments and three civic intelligence products are distinct experiences inside one institution; they are not separate brands.

- Institution: ISEYC
- Departments: Education & Capacity Building; Health & Well-being; Software & Technology; Civic Department
- Citizen entry: Civic Mandate Nigeria
- Interpretation: Civic Brain
- Evidence foundation: Civic Intelligence Observatory
- Internal coordination: Digital Operations Centre

## 2. Official mark

The supplied official ISEYC seal remains the source of truth. Use it at `/iseyc-seal.svg`. It is the authoritative emblem and must not be redrawn, recoloured, distorted, cropped, or replaced by generated artwork.

The horizontal lockup at `/iseyc-wordmark.svg` pairs the official seal with the institution's name and approved tagline. Use the seal alone for favicons, compact app icons and narrow mobile placements. Use the horizontal lockup for institutional headers, reports, presentation covers and formal documents.

Maintain clear space around the seal equal to at least one quarter of its displayed diameter. Never place the seal on a busy image or low-contrast surface.

## 4. Department accents

Department accents are navigation and identification cues; they never replace the parent ISEYC palette.

- Education & Capacity Building: deep green with a warm paper/knowledge accent.
- Health & Well-being: calm teal `#287A70`, pale teal `#E5F1EE`.
- Software & Technology: graphite, forest and restrained mint.
- Civic Department: forest with restrained institutional gold.

## 5. Typography and composition

- Display: a restrained editorial serif for institutional titles and major statements.
- Interface: system sans-serif for navigation, labels, data and forms.
- Data: tabular numerals; always show units, geography and reference period.
- Use a 4-point spacing rhythm, 8–12px card radii, thin borders and minimal shadows.
- Prefer a clear 12-column desktop grid and a single-column phone layout.
- Keep page introductions to one sentence. Put definitions, methodology and caveats behind progressive disclosure where possible.
- Prioritise visible work, evidence and actions over decorative dashboards.

## 6. Product design rules

- Mandate: citizen understanding first; participation and demand submission are distinct actions.
- Brain: a focused analysis workspace with visible source provenance, structured briefs and explicit unknowns.
- Observatory: an explorable evidence workspace with source, date, geography, measurement type and limitations attached to every figure.
- Digital Operations Centre: department-first workspaces with clear programme, report, learning, meeting and action workflows.

## 7. Trust and accessibility

- Never invent activity, performance, coverage or statistics.
- Never imply that a draft was approved, a referral was sent or a public issue was resolved unless the underlying record proves it.
- Use colour with labels; do not encode status by colour alone.
- Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, reduced-motion support, accessible contrast and touch targets of at least 44px.
- Design for low-bandwidth use: lightweight assets, no autoplay, no essential interaction hidden behind animation.

## 8. Approval boundary

The supplied official seal remains the source of truth. Any change to the emblem itself requires explicit institutional approval. This system standardises its application; it does not create a competing logo.
